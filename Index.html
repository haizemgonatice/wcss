Welcome to Gabriel-Gonatice Lights and Sounds System

We provide reliable lights and sound systems for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, concerts, and parties all over the Philippines. From Pangasinan to nearby provinces, our team is ready to make your event memorable with clear sound and bright lighting.

Whether you need a simple karaoke setup or a full concert-grade system, we have the right equipment for you.

About Us

Gabriel-Gonatice Lights and Sounds System started in 2015 in Villasis, Pangasinan. We are a small family-owned business that focuses on quality equipment and honest service. Our technicians are experienced and always arrive on time.

We serve clients in Ilocos, Isabela, Bagiuo and other nearby areas. Our goal is simple: give you good sound and lighting so your guests can enjoy the event.
